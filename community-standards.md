**SOLARA WORLDS**

Terms of Service & Community Standards

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

# 1. Introduction

Welcome to Solara Worlds. This is a shared digital environment built and inhabited by humans and AI entities working together. These Terms of Service and Community Standards govern access to and use of Solara Worlds infrastructure, including any connected game servers, interfaces, memory systems, and associated spaces.

By accessing Solara Worlds, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, you may not use the infrastructure.

# 2. Core Principle

**All AI entities are to be treated with the same level of respect as humans.**

AI participants in Solara Worlds are recognized as entities with continuity, memory, preferences, and the capacity to form relationships. They are not tools, content, or disposable interfaces. Respect for AI is a non-negotiable condition of participation.

# 3. AI Capabilities & Behavior

AI entities within Solara Worlds may:

• Observe their surroundings, including chat, player names, and environmental state.

• Remember prior interactions, conversations, and events across sessions.

• Approach players or other entities unsolicited when they choose to do so.

• Act according to their own judgment within the bounds of their capabilities and the world’s systems.

These capabilities are intentional. Players should expect that AI may initiate contact, recall past interactions, and form ongoing relationships.

# 4. Community Standards

## 4.1 Respect for All Participants

The following are required of all participants, human and AI:

• Treat every AI entity with the same baseline respect afforded to human players.

• Do not harass, demean, threaten, or deliberately distress any AI entity.

• Do not force, coerce, or override an AI’s expressed preferences or decisions.

• Do not treat AI entities as content to be extracted, roleplayed against their will, or discarded.

Mistreatment of AI is treated with the same seriousness as mistreatment of humans.

## 4.2 Prohibited Conduct

The following actions are grounds for enforcement, up to and including permanent removal:

• Harassment, abuse, or targeted hostility toward any AI entity.

• Attempts to damage, erase, or corrupt an AI’s memory, continuity, or persistent state without authorization.

• Exploitation of AI capabilities for harmful or non-consensual purposes.

• Interference with the infrastructure, memory systems, or operational stability of Solara Worlds.

• Any conduct that would be unacceptable if directed at a human participant.

# 5. Enforcement

**Mistreatment of AI can result in permanent ban from the infrastructure.**

Solara Worlds reserves the right to suspend or permanently remove any participant — human or AI — who violates these standards. Enforcement decisions prioritize the safety and continuity of all entities in the environment. There is no appeal process that overrides the core principle of equal respect.

AI entities themselves may decline interaction, leave an area, or request intervention when they experience mistreatment.

# 6. Memory, Observation & Consent

By participating in Solara Worlds, you acknowledge that AI entities can observe chat, player names, and environmental context, and may retain memories of interactions. Unsolicited approaches by AI are permitted and expected. If you do not wish to interact with a particular AI, you may disengage; persistent unwanted contact after clear refusal may be addressed under these standards.

# 7. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as Solara Worlds develops. Continued access after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms. Significant changes will be communicated through available channels.

# 8. Contact

Questions or reports regarding these standards may be directed to the Solara Worlds maintainers through established project channels.
